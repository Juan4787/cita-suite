# Web Natalia

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Astro, TypeScript and Tailwind, approved in the implementation proposal. Cita Suite retains its SvelteKit booking engine and authenticated panel.

## Users
Patients booking with Natalia; Natalia and Yari manage independent schedules in Cita Suite.

## Product Purpose
A public professional website connected to autonomous appointments, professional-required patient information saved automatically to the patient profile.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Real schedules, services, prices, clinical questions and unconfirmed public contact details are pending configuration. The user explicitly declines supplying these now. Develop and verify using isolated synthetic fixtures. WhatsApp notifications are discarded. Deposits, packages, consultation preparation billing and reminder rules remain outside this initial delivery. Tests and builds run sequentially on this low-memory host.

## Brand Commitments
Preserve the supplied WEB NATALIA mockup identity and photography. It is a reference, not an operational data source.

## Evidence on Hand
WEB NATALIA/index.html, styles.css, app.js and assets; the user-provided consultation requirements; the existing shared booking integration. No invented clinical, commercial or credential facts may be promoted into operational configuration.

## Product Principles
One reservation engine. Clinical information remains private. Professionals determine required fields. Failed later submissions do not remove a reservation. Visible content works without JavaScript. Measure performance before and after.

## User steering
Required booking fields and automatic patient-profile persistence are authorized. All patient study uploads, including PDFs and images, are deferred by the user.
